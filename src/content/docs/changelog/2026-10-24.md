## PlusEMU v1.0.24

### WIRED

* Link messages and dataframes run with the sender, including in another open room. A link can hear its own message. A loop runs 10 times.
* Empty messages are delivered. Missing text shows as `null`. Shared and imported variables keep the `_=` fallback.
* A link from another room changes state, shows the speaker bubble, then runs its stacks. The Link Speaker shows that bubble on its tile.
* The link editors open, save and refuse a bad save like Habbo.
* Move User and Move Furni in one stack are sent together. A chair stack moves one piece at a time, and a block cannot land on the chair.
* Teleport To fires Walks On before the user lands. Teleport to Room works when both linkers are in the same room.
* Place Temporary Furni can place a copy under a user when Move through users allows it.
* Rollers run after steps. Walks On and Walks Off fire together when the user lands. A rolled bot gets the same updates as a user.
* Give Variable shows its effect again even when the value stays the same. The variable list is sent in one packet. Very long text-connector keys work.
* A leaderboard updates when you place, move or pick up a text connector on its tile.
* Leaves Room can use the leaving user's variables until a delay. Delayed effects keep running after that user leaves.
* Delays longer than 120 pulses are refused, like Habbo. Older boxes run at 120.
* Show Message is limited per player: 10 lines in 5 seconds and 40 in 30 seconds.
* Bot Follow stays on when the user leaves and comes back. A stopped bot finishes its walk. A bot with a hand item picks a random close tile.
* Players who enter late still see highlight and visibility effects.
* A click turns you only when your direction changes. A click on a full tile does not stop your walk.
* Hidden pyramids flip on their own, start raised, and do not save the flip.

### Large rooms

Thanks to Leet for reporting the performance issues in large rooms with a lot of variables and selectors.

* Reading one variable for the whole room took 203 ms. It now takes 18 ms.
* A "furni with variable" selector over the whole room made 15,002 database lookups. It now makes 5. Without the database, that selector took 75 ms and now takes 26 ms.
* A "furni by type" selector with the whole room as its source took 350 ms. It now takes 9 ms.
* A filtering variable selector over 20 furni read 5,000 variables. It now reads 20.

### Staff

* `:takebadge <username> <badge>` removes a badge from an online user and updates the room if they were wearing it.
