**Versions:** PlusEMU 1.0.28, 1.0.29, 1.0.30 and 1.0.31, Volt 1.0.4 to 1.0.9, Volt Renderer 1.0.2, 1.0.3 and 1.0.4, installer 2.3.0 and 2.3.1. Atom CMS and Atom Nuxt changed on 18 November 2026, without a version number.

## Highlights

- **Camera in the installer.** New hotels can take photos at once. Existing hotels get the camera with the next `plusemu-update`. See [camera](/features/camera/).
- **Furni-Matic.** The recycler works like Habbo's Furni-Matic, with 8 slots, a Duckets price, a waiting time and Habbo's prizes. See [Furni-Matic](/features/economy/#furni-matic).
- **Pets like Habbo.** Pet levels, commands, food, drinks, nests, horse riding, pet packages and monster plants follow Habbo's rules. See [pets](/features/pets/).
- **New room settings.** Room owners choose the door tile action, idle sleep, idle kick and pet chat. See [room settings](/features/rooms/#room-settings).
- **Reward tracks.** Prizes use Habbo's format and can be furni, badges, currency, pets, bots or habbicons. The Habboween 2026 track gives Letters. See [reward tracks](/features/reward-tracks/).
- **MariaDB 11.5 and newer.** PlusEMU now installs and runs on new MariaDB versions. See [MariaDB versions](/getting-started/migrations/#mariadb-versions).
- **Volt looks like AIR.** The catalogue, chests, navigator room settings, inventory, purse, buttons and Furni-Matic windows now match the AIR client.

## Before you update

- **Update PlusEMU 1.0.31, Volt 1.0.9 and Volt Renderer 1.0.4 together.** `plusemu-update` does this for you. Volt 1.0.9 already contains Volt Renderer 1.0.4. The server and the client changed the reward track prizes, the pet catalogue pages and the habbicon files together, so an old client does not work well with the new server.
- **Migrations.** PlusEMU 1.0.30 runs migrations 131 to 140 and 142. PlusEMU 1.0.31 runs migration 141 and migrations 143 to 162. They run at startup, and PlusEMU writes a full database backup first. See [database migrations](/getting-started/migrations/).
- **Removed columns.** Migration 131 removes data that nothing reads any more: the column `users.vip`, the columns `GiftsGiven`, `GiftsReceived`, `quest_progress` and `forum_posts` of `user_statistics`, the column `room_models.public_items`, and the tables `housekeeping_log`, `housekeeping_online_peaks` and `furni_editor_log`. If your own website or tools read them, change those tools first.
- **Database collation.** Migration 162 gives every table and text column the collation `utf8mb4_unicode_ci`. On most hotels it changes nothing. A hotel whose tables drifted to another collation gets those tables rebuilt, which can take a while on large tables. Update such a hotel in a quiet moment. See [MariaDB versions](/getting-started/migrations/#mariadb-versions).
- **Habbicons.** Set `habbicons.asset.hash` to `habbo-225e1f8a` in `renderer-config.json` after the client update, or the zombie habbicons do not show. `plusemu-update` keeps your own value of this setting, so change it by hand. See [habbicons](/getting-started/volt-client/#habbicons).
- **Atom CMS client buttons.** After you update Atom CMS, the home, reload, fullscreen and online count buttons on the client page are off. Set `enable_client_buttons` to `1` to show them again. See [Atom CMS client page](/getting-started/configuration/#atom-cms-client-page).

## PlusEMU 1.0.28 to 1.0.31

### Camera

- Every release now contains the camera renderer in the `CameraRenderer` folder.
- The renderer can read the installer's hotel files directly with `CAMERA_ASSET_LAYOUT=hotel-files`.
- Room ads show in camera photos.
- The camera service can also draw avatar images for the hotel website.

### Furni-Matic

- The Furni-Matic opens with Habbo's settings on a hotel that never set it up: 8 slots, 10 Duckets, 10 seconds between two uses and Habbo's prize list (migration 150). A hotel that set up its own recycler keeps its setup.
- The Furni-Matic gives Habbo's Mystery Box. The box cannot be recycled, traded or sold on the Marketplace (migration 158).
- A prize can be a speech bubble style. Opening the box gives the style to the player.
- Recycling and opening a box send the same messages as on Habbo.

### Rooms

- New room settings: door tile, idle sleep, idle kick and mute all pets (migration 137).
- A room that is waking up stays open while a user enters it.
- A room background (room ad) accepts any whole number as `offsetZ`. A newly placed background toner works at once.
- Room ads keep their place when you save their settings.

### Pets

- Pet energy, happiness, levels, commands and experience follow Habbo. Pets learn commands by level (migration 145).
- Pets eat pet food, drink from water bowls and troughs, sleep in nests and play with the pet ball.
- Pets greet their owner with Habbo's welcome lines (migration 161). Pets also chat on their own and say a level-up line for their type.
- Owners can let other users breed with their pet (migration 155).
- Horses: horse products work as in Habbo, also straight from the inventory. Only a saddled horse can be ridden. Riders get the riding effect of the saddle, and they cannot sit on seats or beds. A removed saddle goes back to the owner.
- Pet packages, such as "Your first Habbo Cat", open like on Habbo. The player names the pet, and the package becomes a nest with the pet on it. Dino eggs and cow boxes hatch a pet of a random breed.
- Monster plants: players plant seeds, the plants grow in 48 hours, and players treat them like on Habbo. The pet info window and the inventory show growth, wellbeing and rarity (migration 146).
- The bunny and chick pages show in the catalogue (migration 151), and pet palettes show for every pet offer.

### Reward tracks and quests

- Reward track prizes use Habbo's format (migration 141). Furni, badge, currency, pet, bot and habbicon prizes can be claimed.
- New Habboween 2026 reward track with Letter prizes (migration 144). Monster plant tasks count toward it.
- The zombie habbicons collection (migration 153). Players get these habbicons as prizes. They cannot be bought.

### Furniture and items

- Furniture names match Habbo (migration 159). Only names that still had placeholder text change.
- Furniture definitions, new furniture and clothes from November 2026, and Habbo's catalogue pages and offers.
- Vending machines give Habbo's hand items and effects, and users can walk while a machine dispenses (migrations 143 and 156).
- Credit furni have their credit value (migrations 154 and 157). `CFC_` credit furni cannot be redeemed for credits. See [credit furni](/features/economy/#credit-furni).
- The snowboard slope and jump work, with jump tricks (migration 148).
- Football, the hockey puck, Freeze and Battle Banzai work like on Habbo.

### Database

- PlusEMU installs and runs on MariaDB 11.5 and newer (migration 162).
- Forum posts, club purchases and SnowStorm token purchases work on MariaDB 11.6 and newer.
- Marketplace purchases and cancellations no longer clash, and the Marketplace keeps a sales history.
- Scheduled currency rewards are saved before the player sees them.

### Other fixes

- Group room rights stay in step with the group.
- Staff can take back a role with their own weight that they gave.
- The landing page shows the badge rank, the bonus rare and the current campaign.

## Volt 1.0.4 to 1.0.9 and Volt Renderer 1.0.2 to 1.0.4

### New

- The `:fps` chat command sets the highest frame rate, like in the AIR client. The client now draws at most 60 frames per second by default. See [frame rate](/getting-started/volt-client/#frame-rate).
- The Furni-Matic: fill the slots by dragging furni from the inventory, start the machine and open the box, like in AIR. Volt reads the number of slots from `recycler.number_of_slots`.
- Monster plant breeding windows, the pet revival potion and the "Name your pet" dialog.
- Horse products can be used straight from the inventory.
- Chat style choice shows only the styles a player owns, and chat style prizes show in the reward track.
- The zombie habbicons, in Habbo's own `habbicons.json` format.
- When a player logs out in the client, the client tells the hotel website.
- The camera draws avatar images for the hotel website.

### Looks like AIR

- Catalogue: window position, colours, quantity box, prices, offer count badge, purchase confirmation and the Builders Club frame.
- Chests: the chest window, chest settings, capacity and Wired upgrade windows, and the chest rules.
- Navigator: the room settings window on every tab, the navigator window and the tab captions.
- Inventory, trade window, infostand, purse, hotel view, notifications, trophy plaque, credit exchange window and the reward track prize tiles.
- Buttons and text in the Volter font, and the colours of window skins.

### Fixes

- The background toner works like in AIR.
- The chat keeps going while the browser tab is hidden.
- Room backgrounds sort with the other room images like in AIR.
- Habbicons are read from the bottom of their image sheet, like in AIR.
- Freeze lives show above the avatar.

## Installer 2.3.0 and 2.3.1

- **2.3.0:** the installer sets up the camera service. New hotels can take photos at once. Run `sudo plusemu-update --install-camera` (Windows: `plusemu-update -InstallCamera`) to add the camera to a hotel that is already on the newest PlusEMU. See [camera](/features/camera/).
- **2.3.1:** on an installed hotel, `--remote-database` now changes only the database access. Before, it also reset Atom CMS, the client and the emulator. See [database access](/getting-started/configuration/#database-access).
- New hotels get `habbicons.asset.hash` set to `habbo-225e1f8a`, `recycler.number_of_slots` set to `8` and `image.library.dailytasks.url` in the client settings.

## Atom CMS and Atom Nuxt

- The client page keeps the game running while the player opens the website. The website opens on top of the client, and a "Return to hotel" button shows the client again.
- When a player logs out in the client, the website logs them out in every open tab.
- The client buttons are off by default. The new website setting `enable_client_buttons` turns them on.
