**Versions:** PlusEMU v2026.10.15 to v2026.10.17-1c63590, Volt 3.5.0 (builds bf5e21c to f89c754), Volt Renderer 2.1.0 (builds a649276 to fb4d690), installer 1.2.0 and 1.2.1.

On 15 October the project went public on DevBest. In these three days most of the work went into WIRED again, with each fix checked side by side with real Habbo. There is also Builders Club, the official Wired catalogue and support for Habbo's own AIR client.

## Highlights
- **Public launch.** PlusEMU, Volt and the installer are open to everyone. PlusEMU, the installer and the migrator now use the GPL-3.0 licence, like Volt.
- **Builders Club.** You can buy a membership in the catalogue, like HC. The catalogue mirrors Habbo's, with a warehouse and recolourable blocks.
- **Official Wired catalogue.** Every Wired page has Habbo's offers, order and prices, plus the new `:chooser` disabler.
- **AIR client support.** Habbo's AIR client can connect with its launcher, handshake and game data. Packets match AIR `WIN63-202610151745-318700621`.
- **More than 100 Wired fixes,** for timing, movement, variables, leaderboards, bots and games.
- **Easier installs.** The installer makes admin passwords that Atom CMS accepts and shows your database login at the end.

## PlusEMU v2026.10.15 to v2026.10.17

PlusEMU made one dated release for every merge in this period. The last one is v2026.10.17-1c63590.

### WIRED timing and movement
- Teleports, avatar moves, unfreeze, timers and box flashes happen on Habbo's room ticks. Moves and flashes are sent together with the effect that caused them.
- Box flashes go out in the same pass as the firing, together with the furni states the effects wrote. Chat flashes go out with the chat.
- Walks On and Walks Off fire on the announced step, like on Habbo.
- The 500 ms timer, At Given Time and the 5 second timer count on the room's 500 ms grid, like on Habbo.
- Fast Queue tiles no longer jump back and forth while they carry an avatar. Log boxes and variable boxes no longer split a queue pulse.
- A furni that ends where it started sends no movement.
- Flee, Relative Move, Chase, Furni to Furni and User to Furni move and stack like on Habbo. Carry Users does not carry a user who is walking.
- A Move/Rotate User push on a walking user replaces the step in progress.
- Turning, chasing and direction snapping match Habbo, including diagonals and furni with only some directions.
- A Wired user move shows Habbo's short `wf` status.
- Without Execute in Order, Match to Snapshot and Show Message run after the effects they depend on.
- The random add-on avoids recent effects in the right order, so effects alternate.

### WIRED variables
- Give, Change and Remove Variable save correctly, including global variables and boxes with no chosen variable.
- Definition boxes flash when a box writes their variable and show the last kind of write. Echoes write through to their source.
- Variable Changed uses the changed user as the triggering user and fires for sub-variables and projectiles.
- Built-in variables have Habbo's text connectors and flags, so for example a direction shows as "2 (E)".
- Time Utilities parts show as sub-variables. Time parts before 1970 work.
- `@level`, `@handitem` and `@can_move_freely` can be read and written. Level-up variables use the hotel's values.
- The inspection tab can read and write an object's variables.

### WIRED leaderboards, bots and games
- Leaderboards can use any stacked user variable. Rows update every 10 seconds. Text, times and ids match Habbo.
- Bot Reached Avatar and Bot Reached Furni fire with the bot as the triggering user, at the right moment.
- Bot Talk, Bot Talk To Avatar, Bot Teleport and bot effects work like on Habbo. Bot clothes are saved like Habbo saves them.
- Bought bots use the "Bot Rentable" chat bubble. A database migration moves existing bots to it.
- Habbo's team effects and team chat bubble are used. Effects come back after unfreeze and are cleared when you leave.
- Game counters start and end games and tick like on Habbo.
- Team score and rank conditions check each player's own team.
- Kick User whispers a notice when it skips the room owner.

### WIRED chests and temporary furni
- Wired chests, Wired trades and their notices use Habbo's official AIR protocol.
- Give Currency and Give Furni need an upgraded Wired chest of the right kind, like on Habbo.
- Chest conditions pass when there is no chest and refuse a picked furni that is not a chest.
- Place Furni refuses templates that cannot be copied. Everyone in the room sees a notice when a copy cannot be placed.
- Wired copies get Habbo's temporary id range. Users with rights can move and use them, and the owner can pick them up.
- Selectors can deliver copies, and Remove Temporary Furni removes them like on Habbo.

### WIRED editor and monitor
- The Wired catalogue matches Habbo: pages, order, offers, prices and currencies. New Additions has the Web API box, the `:chooser` disabler, the Leaderboard add-on, WIRED Label and the six Variable FX boxes.
- A new Wired Archive page holds the retired boxes, with Habbo's page text. Wired boxes that Habbo does not sell stay on a separate page.
- New `:chooser` disabler furni. When it is on, only users with rights can open `:chooser`.
- Editor sources and defaults match Habbo's editors, including the transaction and contract editors.
- The Wired monitor tab shows room stats and error rows, and users with Wired modify rights can clear it.
- Users get their Wired permissions on room entry and again when their rights change.
- A room whose texts use `$(trigger_text)` shows Habbo's notice on entry.
- The variables Web API uses Habbo's public routes. Its documentation page is at `/api/public/api-docs/`.
- Selectors show their resting colour after a save. Filters run after the other selectors in the stack.
- Says Something keeps the first 200 characters. Chat capture and text connectors read text like Habbo.
- Show Message is limited to 10 messages per 5 seconds per room.
- Users By Name allows 20 names and 1000 characters, like the editor. Old saved Wired setups load again.
- Smaller fixes: Date Matches, time conditions, wall furni in snapshots, quantifiers, Click User on bots, the picks selector, signal chains and the execution limit.

### Builders Club and catalogue
- You can buy a Builders Club membership from three Memberships pages, like on Habbo.
- The Builders Club catalogue mode works again, with Habbo's full Builders Club catalogue tree.
- Furni that Habbo only lists in its furnidata now have a Builders Club offer too.
- Habbo's 69 recolourable Builders Club blocks are added.
- New `:pickallbc` command. It returns all Builders Club furni in your room to the warehouse. `:pickall` now leaves Builders Club furni alone.
- A room with Builders Club furni from a user without membership is hidden, like on Habbo. The client warns you before a placement hides the room.

### Rooms, furni and chat
- Chat allows 200 characters.
- Chat flooding works like Habbo's: fast extra lines are dropped, with no mute.
- You can place and move furni onto the tile you stand on.
- You lie on the side of a double bed that you click.
- Horses carry their rider on teleports, rollers and Wired moves. A rider first walks up to the horse to mount it.
- You cannot sit or lie down while swimming. The riding effect stays on water and skate tiles.
- Signs expire, the sit status matches Habbo, and used furni update their state correctly.
- The colour tile has its seven colours again. The Master of Words achievement shows in the achievements window.

### AIR client, protocol and stability
- Header ids match AIR `WIN63-202610151745-318700621`.
- Room, user, furni, pet, camera, catalogue, navigator, messenger, group, moderation, badge, quest and Wired packets use Habbo's layouts.
- The AIR client can finish the handshake. Create a key with `"Plus Emulator" --generate-rsa-key <file>` and set it with `PLUS_RSA_PRIVATE_KEY`, `PLUS_RSA_PRIVATE_KEY_FILE` or `Encryption:RsaPrivateKeyFile`.
- The web server serves `/api/client/air.json`, AIR launcher tickets and Flash game data, set up in the new `AirClient` config section.
- Fixed a crash where one bad connection could take the whole hotel offline.
- `:give`, `:givebadge`, `:push`, `:superpush` and `:pull` no longer disconnect the staff member who uses them.
- The camera now renders photos with the Volt client's own camera page, so new furni and effects show in photos. The page folder is set with `CAMERA_PAGE_ROOT`.

### Database
- Release downloads now include a complete `FreshInstall.sql` and the `Database/Migrations` folder.
- New migrations for the Wired catalogue, the Wired Archive text, the Master of Words achievement, bot chat bubbles, AIR launcher tokens and Builders Club.

## Volt 3.5.0 and Volt Renderer 2.1.0

These are the first builds with the name Volt, and the first Volt Renderer releases.

### New
- **Builders Club:** the membership pages, the Builders Club catalogue mode, the warehouse and placing furni from the Builders Club.
- **Recolourable furniture:** a catalogue page with a centred preview, the recolour window and Habbo's infostand layout.
- The "67" avatar expression.
- Wired menu shortcuts `:var` and `:inspect`.
- Chat history uses the real chat bubbles.
- The `:chooser` window follows the room's chooser disabler.
- The variables explorer talks to Habbo's public variables Web API.
- The camera page is built together with the client.

### Wired editor
- The editors look and work like AIR: controls, skins, dropdowns, delay, reference and label settings.
- The Change and Give Variable editors and the variable picker tree match AIR.
- The advanced settings are built like Habbo's editor.
- Invert asks for confirmation when Filter is off. Animation Time shows the chosen time.
- The client ships Habbo's Wired texts and Wired menu texts, so fresh hotels show no raw text keys.
- The chest window, Wired upgrade flow and Wired trade window match AIR.
- WIRED Labels can be placed and keep their bubble after a reconnect.
- `:wired` opens or closes right away when your rights change.

### Fixes
- Effect animations play smoothly again, and furni animations keep running when their state changes.
- Avatars lie at the right depth in beds. A horse rider is drawn between the horse's head and body.
- Wired glides no longer jump between updates.
- Temporary and Builders Club furni no longer fly into your inventory.
- Wired copies show like on Habbo in the infostand and chooser.
- Lamps no longer show a black box in the inventory preview.
- The user and furni choosers use AIR's layout and filters.
- Sit, stand and effect options in your own avatar menu follow swimming and riding like on AIR.
- Badge popups and rarity tags match Habbo.
- The chat input allows 200 characters.
- The selected catalogue category fills the full width. The toolbar and badges draw correctly in Safari and Firefox.
- Packets match the latest Habbo build.

## Installer and tools

- **Installer 1.2.0:** new hotel files pack with the October Wired boxes, 174 more Wired texts, 1,050 new clothing parts and 372 new badges.
- **Installer 1.2.1:** admin passwords now always include the symbols Atom CMS needs. Running the installer again repairs a bad saved password if setup is not finished yet.
- At the end, the installer shows your MariaDB host, port, database, username and password. The README and setup guide explain how to reach the database.
- The hotel files pack now has the room layout pictures for the room creation window. The step numbers on the setup guide page show as round badges again.
- **Database migrator (first work):** the tool that moves an Arcturus (Morningstar) hotel to Plus keeps your data safer. It checks for problems first, keeps a recovery archive and converts more Wired, also from Polaris-based hotels.
