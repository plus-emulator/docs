**Versions:** PlusEMU v2026.10.08-2d680d5, v2026.10.13-dadd306 and v2026.10.14-2509a7f, Volt client 3.5.0 (builds b2ddc85, ed6c6fa and dd63c66, then still named "Octane client"), hotel files 1.1.0, first installer changes.

This week the hotel moved to the habbo.com catalogue and achievements, and the client and server switched to AIR packet ids. The client was renamed from Octane to Volt and lost its custom extras, so it now only has what the official client has.

## Highlights
- **The habbo.com catalogue.** New hotels ship with habbo.com's catalogue and furni data. The furni data now comes from the database, with the right catalogue offer for each furni.
- **Official achievements.** The achievements window holds exactly habbo.com's achievements, and progress no longer restarts at 0 after each level.
- **AIR packet ids.** PlusEMU and Volt now talk with AIR packet ids, and many messages were fixed to match AIR.
- **Octane is now Volt.** The client and renderer have a new name. Its own login, housekeeping panel and editors are removed.
- **Faster start.** The client caches gamedata and furni data until they change, and logs in while the rest of the app still loads.
- **New room commands.** `:bh` sets the build height, `:hidewired` hides Wired boxes, and `:disablediagonal` is a saved room setting.

## PlusEMU v2026.10.08 to v2026.10.14

### Catalogue and furni
- New hotels ship with the habbo.com catalogue and furni data. A script can import it into an existing hotel.
- Furni data is now stored in the database (migration 60). The emulator builds `FurnitureData.json` from it at `/api/gamedata/furnidata`, with the right catalogue offer for each furni. The infostand buy button and catalogue search now open the right item.
- The furni data file is cached by browsers until it changes, so it no longer downloads on every visit.
- Staff folders in the catalogue are headings that only open, like on habbo.com. Before, they opened an empty page.
- Every rejected gift purchase now gets an answer, so the "Give this gift" button no longer stays stuck.
- The bundle discount request no longer logs a packet error when you open an offer.
- Clothing furni can be redeemed again. Looks are checked against Habbo's current figure data.
- Wallpaper, floor and landscape items can be placed from the inventory again, also after a relog.
- Teleports and hoppers work again when placed or loaded.
- Moodlights stay on after an emulator restart.
- Photos keep their image when used. Migration 73 repairs photos that turned black.

### Currencies, trading and marketplace
- All activity point types are stored in one `user_currencies` table (migration 59). The purse, catalogue, gifts, HC, habbicons and SnowStorm work with any points type.
- Trades save items, balances and the log together. A trade can no longer save only half the exchange.
- Trades offer exactly the items you picked, also LTD items.
- The marketplace works with the AIR client: own offers, buy, redeem, cancel, offer batches and a configurable fee.
- Websites can give rewards through new RCON commands that also work for offline users. A login or late logout can no longer undo these rewards.
- `users.online` is now set on login, so websites can see who is in the hotel.

### Achievements, friends and groups
- The official habbo.com achievements are loaded by migration 76. Badges without a definition are now known, and progress carries over to the next level.
- Online friends see on the friend bar when you unlock an achievement level, like on AIR.
- A bought group forum is linked to its group. It shows the group badge, appears in "My Forums" and opens on double-click.
- Group members get a confirmation when they leave a group.

### Rooms and commands
- **New:** `:bh <height>` places and moves floor furni at an exact height until you type `:bh` again (migration 62).
- **New:** `:hidewired` hides all Wired boxes in the room for everyone. The boxes keep working, and the setting is saved (migration 63).
- `:disablediagonal` is now a saved room setting for the owner and users with rights. It lets avatars walk diagonally between two furni that touch at a corner (migration 75).
- Room owners can move and rotate monsterplants and rentable bots like on AIR.
- Avatars can step off a bed diagonally.
- Avatars glide smoothly on rollers again.
- SnowStorm picks a random arena. Arena voting is removed.
- Two answers to the same room poll at once no longer get stuck.

### Camera
- Avatars that sit, lie or ride a horse are placed correctly in photos.
- The camera uses the current furni data, so new furni are no longer refused as missing.
- Photos are ready faster, also in crowded rooms.

### Moderation
- Staff can read chatlogs of unloaded rooms and of users who are offline.
- Room reports point to the reported room. Closing several tickets at once works.

### Protocol and server
- PlusEMU now uses AIR packet ids directly. Old packet revisions are no longer supported.
- Room rights updates, inventory additions, pet records, user status and crafting requests match AIR.
- Wired packets use the official ids. Wrong furni picks in Wired editors get Habbo's error texts.
- The Echo editor lists the built-in variables, like `@index` and `@direction`.
- A closed connection can no longer freeze the game cycle, which stopped avatars and rooms.

### Database and setup
- Migrations 69 to 72 remove unused tables and columns, convert all tables to InnoDB utf8mb4 and add indexes and foreign keys. Back up your database before you update.
- The custom login, housekeeping panel and catalogue and furni editors are removed from the emulator. The website SSO login, furni data and the badge leaderboard still work.
- The `/api/health` endpoint is removed.

## Volt and Volt Renderer

### New
- The client and renderer are now called Volt and Volt Renderer. `:octane` is now `:volt`, and `:nitro` still works. Hotels with an older `bootstrap.js` can still log in.
- The client's own login, staff editors, hotel view editor and other custom extras are removed. The hotel view shows habbo.com's current reception.
- New moderator tools with the classic layouts, issue browsing and evidence. The toolbar button shows the number of open issues.
- The avatar editor has the Hot Looks and Effects tabs.
- The inventory, trade window and marketplace work like the classic client.
- The friend bar shows friend notifications like on AIR.
- The purse and catalogue show all activity point types with AIR icons and names.
- New icons for the crackables, crafting and collectibles achievement categories.

### Faster start
- The start config comes inside the page, and the app downloads while the start script runs.
- Texts, gamedata and furni data are cached until they change, instead of downloading on every visit.
- The client logs in while the app still loads.
- Photo effects load after the hotel starts, in parallel.
- Avatar, furni and image data load at the same time.

### Fixes
- The catalogue matches AIR: product previews, club gifts, block pages with colour choice, multi-product offers, the front page and search.
- Catalogue navigation follows the clicked item, and selecting a tab again goes back to its first page.
- Furni dropped from the catalogue into a room asks for confirmation.
- Settings, room info, the friends list, name bubbles, furni menus and rentable bot menus match AIR.
- Group and forum windows work like on AIR.
- The achievements window works like AIR's.
- Alt+click move, shift+click rotate and ctrl+click pickup work on furni, bots and monsterplants.
- Room links open the right navigator tab and filter.
- Chat text is sharp on scaled screens. Avatar heads in chat bubbles are sharp and centred.
- Chat shows smileys like `:D` as typed, not as emoji, like on Habbo.
- The chat command popup is removed, like on AIR. The "?" button opens the commands help page.
- The room promotion window and Edit Promo look like the classic client.
- Reward track bubbles show their icons, and reward tracks scroll correctly.
- Wired dialogs use AIR's controls and official texts. The Send Signal editor accepts all antennas.
- Wallpaper, floor and landscape items have their names in the inventory.
- The Earnings window and badge leaderboard look like the classic client.
- The purse, catalogue and toolbar draw correctly in Firefox. Firefox no longer shows "Loading failed".
- A missing marketplace texts file no longer stops the client from starting.
- The camera shows "picture could not be created" when a photo fails, instead of a wrong "too many items" message.
- Crowded rooms render, zoom and animate much faster and use less memory. The renderer now uses PixiJS 8.22.

## Installer

- New installs no longer write settings for the removed custom login, ads and news.
- The installer no longer waits for `/api/health`. Any answer from the emulator's web server counts as started.
- Hotel files 1.1.0 was released on 8 October.
