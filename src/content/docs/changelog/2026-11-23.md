**Versions:** PlusEMU 1.0.38, 1.0.39, 1.0.40 and 1.0.41, Volt 1.0.17, 1.0.18 and 1.0.19, Volt Renderer 1.0.9, installer 2.4.0, Atom CMS 1.6.0.

## Highlights

- **Security fixes in every part.** PlusEMU 1.0.41 closes slow and flooding connections before login, refuses logins from banned addresses, can protect RCON with a secret and checks many room, gift, quest and staff command requests. Volt, Atom CMS and the installer have security fixes too. We recommend that every hotel updates.
- **Hotel language.** Run the installer with `--language de` (Windows: `-Language de`), and the client texts, furniture names and product names come from Habbo's German hotel. Atom CMS uses German as its default language. See [change the hotel language](/getting-started/hotel-language/).
- **Pets like Habbo.** Pets eat Carrot, Kale and Choco-Bunny, and their energy comes back at Habbo's speed. Pets play with every pet toy, the frog dips in the pond and the cat drinks from the milk bowl.
- **Badge displays show their badge** again, with the owner and the date in the right places.
- **Habbo Club achievement.** Buying Habbo Club counts the bought days towards the Habbo Club member achievement, so a first month gives its first level.
- **Room Area Hider and Floor Plan Editor like Habbo.** The hider window looks and works like Habbo's, and "Hide wall items" now hides the wall items in front of the hidden area. The Floor Plan Editor window can be moved again.
- **Faster client start.** The client loads less before the hotel shows and loads windows when they open.

## Before you update

- **Update PlusEMU 1.0.41, Volt 1.0.19 and Volt Renderer 1.0.9 together.** `plusemu-update` does this for you. Volt 1.0.19 already contains Volt Renderer 1.0.9.
- **Migration 175.** PlusEMU 1.0.41 adds the `moderation.user_email` permission and gives it to the roles at security level 7. Other staff no longer see user e-mail addresses. `plusemu-update` and the emulator's start apply the migration.
- **RCON.** `Rcon:AllowedAddresses` takes only IP addresses now. If it lists `localhost`, replace it with `127.0.0.1`. To protect RCON with a secret, set the same value in `Rcon:Secret` (emulator) and `RCON_SECRET` (Atom CMS `.env`).
- **Proxies.** If a proxy other than the local one sits in front of the emulator, add its address to `TrustedProxies` in `Config/config.json`. Otherwise all players share the proxy's address and its connection limit.
- **Atom CMS.** Sensitive website settings now need their own permission. After you update Atom CMS, run these commands in the Atom CMS folder, so that the highest staff rank gets it:

  ```bash
  php artisan migrate --force
  php artisan db:seed --class=HousekeepingPermissionSeeder --force
  ```

## PlusEMU 1.0.38 to 1.0.41

### Security (1.0.41)

- **Connections.** A connection that does not log in in time is closed. Each address may hold at most 20 Flash connections (`Flash:MaxConnectionsPerIp`). A connection that sends too much data, or a malformed packet before login, is closed.
- **Handshake.** The emulator answers the Diffie-Hellman key exchange once per connection, and echoes only the first short `UniqueId` before login.
- **AIR login.** The AIR web endpoints slow down an address that sends wrong tickets or tokens.
- **Real visitor address.** `Flash:TrustedProxies` and `WebServer:TrustedProxies` in `Config/config.json` list the proxies that may pass the visitor's real address. The defaults are `127.0.0.1` and `::1`.
- **IP bans.** A banned address can no longer log in. A value stays banned until its last ban row expires.
- **RCON.** Set `Rcon:Secret` to make every RCON request send the same secret. Atom CMS sends it as `RCON_SECRET`. Malformed old-style commands close the connection. `AllowedAddresses` takes only IP addresses, so `localhost` no longer matches.
- **E-mail addresses.** The moderation tool and `:userinfo` show a user's e-mail address only to staff with the new `moderation.user_email` permission. Migration 175 gives it to the roles at security level 7.
- **Rooms.**
  - A hopper or teleporter lets a user past the door of the next room once.
  - Rights go only to real users, not to the owner, and not past the rights limit. Room mutes do not work on the owner or past the role limit.
  - Only users with rights can edit a sticky note.
  - A repeated room entry from a user who is already in the room is ignored.
  - You can follow only friends who allow it.
  - A room promotion is a paid catalog purchase of the room ad offer.
- **Groups.** A private group cannot be joined directly. Only members can set a group as their favourite.
- **Gifts and trophies.** Gifted furni get the same data as a normal purchase, also when an old gift is opened. Gift messages and trophy texts are cut, escaped and filtered.
- **Text limits.** Whispers and sticky notes are cleaned, cut and filtered like talk. Bot speech is cut to 100 lines of 200 characters. A chat log entry that the database refuses no longer drops the other entries.
- **Quests.** A quest reward is paid once, when its saved progress completes. Saved quest progress loads at login.
- **Help tickets.** A user waits 60 seconds between help tickets. The message is cut to 253 characters and a report keeps at most 100 chat lines. A closed or deleted ticket no longer shows as open to staff.
- **Staff commands.** `:mute`, `:carry` and other commands refuse invalid numbers. `:give` refuses amounts below 1 or above the maximum balance.
- **Wardrobe.** Outfits are saved only to real slots with a valid gender.

### Pets

- Pets eat pet food that has no food values, such as Carrot, Kale and Choco-Bunny. Each bite gives 25 energy and 10 experience, and every pet type may eat it.
- A resting pet gets back its full energy in one hour. A level 1 pet gets a point every 30 seconds, and higher levels get points faster. Before, every pet got a point every 40 seconds.
- A pet can no longer be placed on a tile that a Room Area Hider hides. The owner gets the "You can not place your pet there" message.
- Every pet plays with the Rubber Ball (`toy1`), the Spinning Top (`toy2`), `pet_ufo_toy` and the Funky Chicken (`pet_puppy_toy`), not only with the pet ball. (1.0.39)
- The pond is a toy for the pet types in its custom params (the frog and the turtle). A frog dips in it. (1.0.39)
- A cat drinks from the milk bowl. The milk bowl never runs out. (1.0.39)
- Pets say their own playing lines too, for example the frog's "Anyone up for a game of croak-ay?". (1.0.39)

### Furni

- Badge displays show their badge. Saved badge displays keep their badge, owner and date.
- A new Room Area Hider starts with "Hide wall items" ticked.
- With "Hide wall items" ticked, a Room Area Hider that is on hides the wall items in front of the tiles it hides. A wall item that moves into or out of that part disappears or comes back. (1.0.40)

### Habbo Club

- Every bought Habbo Club day counts towards the Habbo Club member achievement (`ACH_HC`) at once.

### Fixed (1.0.41)

- Opening a profile keeps the user's live stats.
- The first scheduled currency reward comes after a full interval.

## Volt 1.0.17 to 1.0.19 and Volt Renderer 1.0.9

### New

- The client loads less before the hotel shows, starts loading earlier, and loads windows when they first open.
- The Habbo Journey and Habboween reward track bars show as soon as the hotel shows.

### Changed

- The Floor Plan Editor window can be moved by its title bar again. It has Habbo's "?" button, which opens the Builders Club help page, and Habbo's subtitle.
- The Room Area Hider window uses Habbo's layout, buttons, texts and grey hints. It greys out while the hider is on, and Select Area stays disabled until you have chosen an area.
- The badge display window shows the owner name and the date in the right fields.
- The close button of Habbo-style windows, such as the area hider, alert and pet package windows, sits where Habbo has it.
- The group furni menu fits its buttons, centres its title and cuts off long group names.
- Underlined native text is drawn like in the AIR client.
- The open drop menus of Wired boxes no longer draw a box behind their rows.
- `CFC_` credit furni no longer open the credit exchange window.

### Security

- Links in hotel texts and `openWebPage` open only web addresses.
- The chat history is kept per account, and HTML in hotel texts is checked more strictly.

## Installer 2.4.0

- The AIR client routes send the visitor's real address to the emulator, so per-address limits and bans see the player and not the proxy. Setup leaves old AIR blocks alone when it cannot replace them safely.
- The installer and `plusemu-update` accept `--language <code>` (Windows: `-Language <code>`). The codes are `en`, `de`, `nl`, `fr`, `es`, `it`, `fi`, `tr` and `br`. `PLUSEMU_LANGUAGE` does the same on an install.
- The choice is saved, so a new run and `plusemu-update` keep the language and install the newest language pack. Run `sudo plusemu-update --language de` (Windows: `plusemu-update -Language de`) to change the language of a hotel that is already installed. Use `en` to go back to English.
- When the language changes, the installer writes the new furniture names into the `furniture` table. Furniture names you edited are replaced.
- Catalogue pages, emulator messages and badge names on the website stay English.

## Atom CMS 1.6.0

- Password reset links go only to the e-mail address stored on the account.
- The current password check allows five tries per minute, and each address gets at most three reset mails per link.
- The install wizard does nothing until the installation key is entered.
- Website settings are checked when they are saved and when they are used. Sensitive settings, such as `housekeeping_url` and `nitro_path`, need a permission that only the highest staff rank may manage.
- Shop rank items cannot give a staff rank or a lower rank. Staff uploads are named by their real image type. The stock shop no longer adds a VIP rank item.
- The registration webhook cannot mention anyone on Discord.
- Atom CMS sends `RCON_SECRET` with every request to the emulator's RCON.
- `web-auth/webauthn-lib` is updated to 5.3.17.
