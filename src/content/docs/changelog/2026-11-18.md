**Versions:** PlusEMU 1.0.32 to 1.0.37, Volt 1.0.10 to 1.0.16, Volt Renderer 1.0.5, 1.0.6, 1.0.7 and 1.0.8. The installer and Atom CMS changed without a new version number.

## Highlights

- **.NET 11.** PlusEMU now runs on .NET 11. The server's processor must support the x86-64-v2 instructions. The installer checks this for you.
- **Latest Habbo AIR build.** Packets match AIR `WIN63-202611201617-120873662`, so the official AIR client can still connect.
- **AIR client in one command.** Run the installer with `--with-air` (Windows: `-WithAir`), and players can use the AIR client. See [connect the AIR client](/getting-started/air-client/).
- **Group invitations.** The new Wired box Invite to Group sends a player an invitation to a group. The player accepts or rejects it in a window.
- **New Wired boxes.** Invite to Group, Remove from Group, Group Membership Changed, User Clicks Context Menu and the Context Menu Item add-on. A room can now add its own items to the menu of a user, pet or bot.
- **Monster plant breeding.** Players breed two of their own monster plants into a seed, and the seed grows into a plant of its rarity.
- **Group furni like Habbo.** Group furni keep their group in the inventory and on the Marketplace, and they show the group colours in the inventory, the room and the catalogue.

## Before you update

- **Update PlusEMU 1.0.37, Volt 1.0.16 and Volt Renderer 1.0.8 together.** `plusemu-update` does this for you. Volt 1.0.16 already contains Volt Renderer 1.0.8. The new AIR build changed the numbers of the network messages, so an old client cannot talk to a new server.
- **Processor.** The .NET 11 emulator needs a processor with the x86-64-v2 instructions. Some old virtual server types do not have them, and then the emulator stops at start. The installer and `plusemu-update` check the processor first. If it is too old, they stop and change nothing. On a virtual server, ask your provider for a newer processor type, for example `host` or `x86-64-v2`. ARM64 servers are not affected.
- **Migrations.** PlusEMU 1.0.33 runs migrations 163 and 164. PlusEMU 1.0.34 runs migrations 166 to 168. PlusEMU 1.0.35 runs migrations 169 and 170. PlusEMU 1.0.36 runs migrations 171 to 173. PlusEMU 1.0.37 runs migration 174. They run at startup, and PlusEMU writes a full database backup first. See [database migrations](/getting-started/migrations/).
- **Group badges.** Migrations 166 and 168 give the badge parts Habbo's ids and Habbo's order in the badge editor. The migrations also change the badge codes of existing groups, so their badges keep their look. A badge part of your own keeps its id, unless a Habbo part needs that id. If you added your own badge parts to `groups_items`, check them in the badge editor after the update.
- **Habbicons.** `plusemu-update` now changes `habbicons.asset.hash` from `default` to `habbo-225e1f8a` for you. Any other value is your own choice, and the update keeps it. If you made your own habbicon folder, start again from a copy of `habbo-225e1f8a`, because the habbicon sets now use Habbo's ids. See [habbicons](/getting-started/volt-client/#habbicons).

## PlusEMU 1.0.32 to 1.0.37

### Server

- PlusEMU runs on .NET 11. The release archives still include the runtime, so you do not install .NET yourself.
- Packets match AIR `WIN63-202611201617-120873662`.
- The server disconnects a client that stops answering its pings. It sends a ping every 30 seconds and disconnects after 3 pings without an answer. Change this with `ConnectionCheck.PingIntervalSeconds` and `ConnectionCheck.MaxUnansweredPings` in `Config/config.json`. A value below 1 uses the default.

### Groups

- New group invitations (migration 171). An invitation counts as the owner's consent, so a player who accepts it joins the group, whatever the group type. A player has at most one invitation per group. A new invitation updates the old one.
- Group badges use Habbo's badge part ids, editor ids and editor order (migrations 166 and 168). Badge symbols from 100 up get the `t` prefix, like on Habbo.
- Group furni keep their group in the inventory and show their group look in the inventory and in the room.
- Bought group furni start in state 0, like on Habbo (migration 167). Older group furni that stored the group id as their state go back to state 0.
- Users can no longer open a group gate by hand. Room controllers can use one-state group furni.
- Group furni show as giftable in the catalogue, like on Habbo, but the server refuses the gift (migration 169).
- A group furni on the Marketplace keeps its group (migration 170). The buyer, or the seller after a cancel, gets the furni with its group.
- Group info shows the owner as a member, marks the viewer's favourite group, and shows the creation date like on Habbo.

### Wired

- New Wired boxes for groups (migration 172): Invite to Group, Remove from Group and the trigger Group Membership Changed. Remove from Group works like the leave button. The trigger fires on every join and leave. A group box only saves a group that the editor owns.
- New Wired boxes for menus (migration 173): the trigger User Clicks Context Menu and the add-on Context Menu Item. The add-on adds items to the menu that opens when you click a user, pet or bot. An item can show or be enabled only for certain users. A click on an item fires the trigger.
- Habbo has shown these boxes but not released them. They are sold on the Wired pages of the catalogue and use the look of placeholder Wired furni. Migration 174 gives them their own names in every client.
- Variable effects accept the Christmas 2026 icons and styles.
- Teleport effects refuse targets outside the room. Teleporter pairs are checked when they are saved and when a user uses them.
- Altitude heights are rounded before they are compared.

### Pets and monster plants

- Players breed two of their own monster plants into a seed, like on Habbo.
- Seeds grow into a plant of their rarity.
- Monster plant breeding gives the breeder rewards like on Habbo.
- Ridden horses jump the bar of every horse jump.
- A pet command no longer leaves a rider floating over the horse.

### Habbicons and catalogue

- Habbicon sets use Habbo's ids (migration 163).
- Only the Toast habbicons are for sale, like on Habbo: the set costs 50 credits and 50 diamonds, and one icon costs 5 credits and 5 diamonds (migration 164). Players earn the Duck, Duck 2 and Frank habbicons. They are not free to claim any more. A price that you changed yourself stays.
- A player without enough credits or diamonds gets the "not enough balance" message.
- An offer that costs points can be a gift only when it costs credits and diamonds, like on Habbo. Offers that cost Duckets can no longer be sent as a gift.

## Volt 1.0.10 to 1.0.16 and Volt Renderer 1.0.5 to 1.0.8

### New

- The group invitation window.
- Editors for the new group and context menu Wired boxes.
- The menu of a user, pet or bot shows the room's own Wired items under the name. A disabled item is grey. A bot without items gets no menu.
- Variable effect pickers use a list with groups, and they show the Christmas 2026 icons and styles.
- The habbicon purchase window shows credits and activity points in separate rows, and it closes when the player does not have enough balance.
- The network message numbers of AIR `WIN63-202611201617-120873662`, the same as the server.

### Looks like AIR

- Group forums: the forum list, threads, messages, the message editor, the settings window and the page numbers.
- Group windows: the group manager, the member list, the member search and the group info badge.
- The group badge editor: Habbo's editor ids, colours in Habbo's order, and badge parts tinted like the Habbo imager.
- The group furni page in the catalogue: price chips, buttons, plain furni icons and the group colours in the purchase confirmation.
- The pet menu and the monster plant menu.
- Text in html windows, and button skins.

### Fixes

- The reward track hides the premium row when the track has no premium setup.
- The status bar style 21 is called "Water", like on Habbo.
- The camera draws avatar images on Habbo's imager canvas.

## Installer

- The installer sets up the AIR client with `--with-air` (Windows: `-WithAir`). The choice is saved, so a new run and `plusemu-update` keep it on. Run `sudo plusemu-update --with-air` (Windows: `plusemu-update -WithAir`) to turn it on for a hotel that is already installed. See [set up with the installer](/getting-started/air-client/#set-up-with-the-installer).
- A new install and an emulator update stop before they change anything when the processor cannot run .NET 11. The message names the missing instructions.
- On Windows, the installer checks the runtime that comes with PlusEMU before it installs it, and it puts the emulator on the hotel drive.
- `plusemu-update` changes `habbicons.asset.hash` from `default` to `habbo-225e1f8a` when the client has that folder.

## Atom CMS

- The website can show avatar images that your hotel draws, through an address like Habbo's avatar imager. See [avatar images](/features/avatar-images/).
- Avatar images work for looks that end with a dot.
