**Versions:** PlusEMU 1.0.26 and 1.0.27, Volt 1.0.1, 1.0.2 and 1.0.3, Volt Renderer 1.0.1, installer 2.1.0, 2.2.0 and 2.2.1.

## Highlights

- **Latest Habbo AIR build.** Packets match AIR `WIN63-202611041140-327405090`, so the official AIR client can still connect.
- **Global home room.** Players without their own home room can start in a room you choose.
- **Database access from your PC.** The installer can open the database to your own IP address, for example for HeidiSQL.
- **SSH key login only.** The installer can turn off SSH password login for you, safely.

## Before you update

- Update PlusEMU 1.0.27, Volt 1.0.3 and Volt Renderer 1.0.1 together. The new AIR build changed the numbers of the network messages, so an old client cannot talk to a new server.
- PlusEMU 1.0.27 runs migrations 132 and 133 when it starts.

## PlusEMU 1.0.26 and 1.0.27

- New server setting `global_home_room` (migration 133). Set it to a room ID, and users without their own home room go to that room when they log in or press "go to home room". A user's own home room still wins. `0` turns it off, and that is the default. Reload it with `:update settings`.
- The stock promotions on the catalogue front page stay up until the end of 2030 (migration 132). Promotions you added or changed keep their end date.
- Staff can give and remove roles up to their own weight. Roles with a higher weight are still refused.
- Wired lifts sitting, stepping and stacked users like Habbo.

## Volt 1.0.1 to 1.0.3 and Volt Renderer 1.0.1

- The client uses the network message numbers of AIR `WIN63-202611041140-327405090`, the same as the server.
- The Marketplace has the offer type drop-down like AIR.
- The Habbo help pages that the client links to are part of the client.
- The HC gift count updates right after a player picks a gift.
- The avatar stands on the mat in the hotel view, and lamp glows show in the furniture chest preview.
- Ctrl and the mouse wheel no longer zoom in the SnowStorm arena.

## Installer 2.1.0 to 2.2.1

- **2.1.0:** `--remote-database <addresses>` opens the database port to these IPv4 addresses only. See [database access](/getting-started/configuration/#database-access).
- **2.2.0:** `--setup-ssh` adds your SSH key and then turns SSH password login off. See [SSH key login](/getting-started/server-protection/#ssh-key-login).
- **2.2.1:** running the installer again never replaces a database that has accounts. Before this fix, a second run, for example with `--remote-database` or `--setup-ssh`, replaced the hotel database with an empty one. If you lost your hotel this way, restore a nightly backup from `/var/backups/plusemu/nightly/`.
